# Original asset workshop

Every visual and sound asset in Willowmere Acres is authored within this repository. No images, sampled audio, font outlines, asset packs, game references, or image-generation services were used.

- `tools/palette.ts` defines exactly 48 original color inks. PNG generation uses integer palette indices, including title art and install icons. Transparent pixels are excluded from palette comparison.
- `tools/pixel.ts` supplies integer raster primitives. `tools/generate.ts` composes roof shingles, timber framing, window boxes, plowed soil, leaf clusters, water ripples, growth stages, rounded animal silhouettes, clothing, hairstyles and expressive portraits from those primitives. A stable integer hash distributes tiny grass/leaf highlights. Character silhouettes are 16 × 24, portraits 48 × 48, trees 48 × 64. The atlas uses one-pixel separation.
- Four original intro illustrations (`intro_city`, `intro_letter`, `intro_arrival`, `intro_farm`) are independently composed in `tools/generate.ts`: a rainy office skyline and desk letter, a botanical-stamped handwritten letter with tea and pressed flowers, an arrival wagon in the village square, and the open cottage gate. The small inscription uses only the project’s own glyph data.
- The title vista is assembled from original landscape geometry and original generated sprites, with hand-positioned farm architecture, crop rows and distant ridgelines. The cloud sprite is available separately for animated parallax. Four water frames and walking frames provide animation.
- `tools/font.ts` contains the hand-authored 5 × 7 pixel alphabet, punctuation and digits with compositional accents for Latin-1. `tools/ttf.ts` builds TrueType binary tables and individual rectangular pixel contours from exactly these glyphs. The browser and bitmap versions share an original source; neither inherits an existing typeface.
- `tools/autotile.ts` normalizes all 256 neighbor masks to the 47 valid blob states. `tools/generate.ts` draws distinct rounded convex edges and concave corners for grass, dirt, tilled earth, water, sand and snow, plus watered soil. Water has four frames for every mask. The matching browser helper selects these same frames in the runtime renderer.
- `tools/maps.ts` writes all nine Tiled-compatible layers for every catalog location: Ground, GroundDetail, Collision, Objects, AbovePlayer, Lights, Warps, Spawns and Triggers. It uses the shared world layout for terrain/buildings, original prop sprites, flower details, natural tree objects and canopy occlusion regions, window/torch lights, catalog NPC schedules, connected destination warps and interaction triggers. Inapplicable layers remain empty in locations where the corresponding feature is absent.
- `src/audio/songs.ts` contains 21 separately composed melodic cells and their rhythmic grids, chord progressions, phrase answers and orchestration. Each expands into 40 four-beat bars (at least 68.57 seconds at the fastest tempo), with contrasting sections before a seamless return. Seven separate short reward melodies are used as jingles.
- `src/audio/engine.ts` synthesizes two variable-duty pulse channels, a triangle bass channel, filtered deterministic noise percussion and an additive soft wave channel using Web Audio. Envelopes, vibrato, arpeggiation, portamento, swing, crossfades, separate mix buses and ducking are implemented directly. `src/audio/sfx.ts` contains 66 original parameterized effects; all are oscillators or generated noise, never recordings.

The generated assets are reproducible with `pnpm gen:assets`; the palette/geometry/content/audio tests verify the resulting files and composition data.
