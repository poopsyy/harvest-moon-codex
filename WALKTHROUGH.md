# Your first fourteen days

Willowmere runs on watered nights, conversations, and manageable plans. Open the field guide from the menu whenever you need the controls. Menus pause the clock. Your first 500G is enough to get started; keep some for another bag of seeds.

| Day | A comfortable plan                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Inspect the tools beside the screen. Clear weeds with the sickle, stones with the hammer, and branches with the axe. Till a small patch with the hoe. Select turnip seeds, sow, and water each planted tile. Save at the diary and sleep at the farmhouse bed. |
| 2   | Water the turnips. Visit Willowmere Square, then Market Lane. The People page shows each neighbor’s current destination; walk there and say hello.                                                                                                             |
| 3   | Water again. Open the journal’s Wren’s pages tab and investigate the farmhouse floorboard clue. Gather a few seasonal finds in Mirebrook Forest.                                                                                                               |
| 4   | Water the young crops. Check the calendar for birthdays and the upcoming Seedling Parade. Refill the watering can at the well when needed.                                                                                                                     |
| 5   | After four watered nights, the first turnips are ready. Harvest, put them in the shipping bin, and plant another batch. Shipping pays overnight.                                                                                                               |
| 6   | Review the night summary. Buy seeds that will mature before Spring ends. Radishes take five watered nights, potatoes six, and cabbage thirteen.                                                                                                                |
| 7   | Keep the field small enough to water comfortably. Find a neighbor you enjoy talking with and offer a harvested crop or forage as a gift. One gift per day and two per week is plenty.                                                                          |
| 8   | Visit the Seedling Parade. Bring flowers if you have them and try the cart activity. Participation is worthwhile even without a perfect result.                                                                                                                |
| 9   | Harvest the next ready turnips. Check your satchel before shipping everything: keep ingredients for requests and favorite gifts.                                                                                                                               |
| 10  | Follow the Riverside path to Bram’s workshop and Rosalind’s ranch. Look at coop and barn prices before buying animals. A coop costs 2,500G, 80 wood, and 30 stone; construction takes three nights.                                                            |
| 11  | Try fishing at the riverside. Follow the fish with the reel window and ease off when the tension rises. Fish have different seasons and habits.                                                                                                                |
| 12  | Visit Mount Tallow and the mine. Break rocks carefully, look for the ladder, and leave enough energy to get home. Early ores begin the path to tool upgrades.                                                                                                  |
| 13  | Check the requests board. A delivery uses three of the requested item and earns payment plus friendship. Buy more seed only if its harvest fits the remaining season.                                                                                          |
| 14  | Celebrate Odalie’s birthday with a visit. Review the calendar, journal, farm supplies, and savings. Decide whether the next fortnight is for a coop, tool upgrades, or a larger garden.                                                                        |

Rain waters crops for you. A dry night does not advance their growth. Crops outside the greenhouse wither when their season ends. Sleep before midnight for full recovery; food and the mountain hot spring can help on a busy day. Use the diary, three manual slots, morning autosave, and JSON export to keep your progress.

# Livestock and home projects

Bram’s workshop lists costs and construction times. Complete a coop before welcoming a chicken, or a barn before welcoming a cow or sheep. Name each animal at purchase. Feed and pet them daily; brush large animals, collect ready produce, and bring animals inside before bad weather. The animal panel shows whether an animal has eaten and whether it needs tonic.

The first house extension adds a kitchen. Recipes list the exact ingredients required. Processing turns eggs, milk, wool, fruit, and ore into more valuable goods; place the corresponding machine and allow each batch time to finish. Farmhouse decoration uses the furniture selector and placement grid. Wallpaper and flooring are separate catalog categories.

# Story guide — spoilers

The journal provides each chapter’s current objective. Progress comes from farming and friendship as well as exploration.

1. **Spring Hearthstone:** Restore the orchard. Upgrade the axe and hammer to Iron, plant three saplings, ship fifty spring crops, and reach three hearts with Bram. Follow the chapter at the Lantern Tree.
2. **Summer Hearthstone:** Catch fifteen fish species, build trust with Corran, and help Lira’s boat repairs. Explore the hidden cove when the chapter opens the way.
3. **Autumn Hearthstone:** Reach floor fifty of the old mine and help Greta face the unfinished story of her friendship with Wren.
4. **Winter Hearthstone:** Help ten neighbors reach three hearts, develop at least three villager stories, and join the Longnight Gathering. The fourth hearthstone comes from the shared hearth.
5. **Lantern Bloom:** Bring all four hearthstones together. From the next spring, the Lantern Bloom Vigil becomes an annual celebration.
6. **Council vote:** Continue supporting the village through Year 3 Spring. Recover at least twelve journal pages to bring Wren home for the vote. Restoring the Lantern Tree is essential to a hopeful outcome; stronger community ties, thriving farming, collections, and completed stories improve the result.

The nine neighbor arcs follow Marta, Dex, Hollis and June, Ansel, Sable, Ivy, Kip, Lira, and Halverd. Choices respect different positive outcomes. Heart events become available through friendship and meeting people at their associated locations.

Ivy, Kip, Ansel, Lira, and Sable are romanceable adults. A Willow Ribbon at eight hearts begins dating. Ten hearts, the second house extension, the restored Winter chapter, and a Hearth Ring prepare a proposal. After marriage, daily routines and family stories continue; a child can join the household after a year.

There is no permanent losing ending. An unfinished restoration delays the valley’s future and leaves time to keep helping. After the vote, you can continue farming and living in Willowmere indefinitely.
