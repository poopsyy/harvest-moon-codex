## Catalog owner (src/data/catalog.ts, schemas, strings/content counts)

Export arrays `crops`, `trees`, `flowers`, `items`, `npcs`, `fish`, `forage`, `minerals`, `recipes`, `processedGoods`, `decor`, `wallpapers`, `floors`, `festivals`, `achievements`, `maps`, `shops`, `buildings`; export `validateCatalog()` and `itemById(id)`.
Common IDs are lowercase snake_case. Seasons `spring|summer|autumn|winter`. Crop rows `{id,name,season,seedPrice,growDays,regrowDays,sellPrice}`. Item rows `{id,name,category,price,description,stamina?}` and every seed id `${crop.id}_seed`. Item price is normal shipping value, seed item price shop cost. Recipe `{id,name,ingredients:{item:string,count:number}[],stamina,price}`. NPC `{id,name,age,pronouns,role,home,birthday:{season,day},romanceable,personality,voice,loved:string[],liked:string[],disliked:string[],hated:string[],color:number,schedule:{hour:number,map:string,x:number,y:number}[]}`. NPC ids odalie,bram,greta,pell,noor,ivy,rosalind,kip,ansel,marta,dex,corran,lira,sable,fenwick,hollis,june,ilse,pip,ozzie,halverd,wren. Map IDs farm, farmhouse, square, market, riverside, hilltop, forest, mountain, beach, mine, store, forge, clinic, inn, workshop, ranch, library, chapel, townhall, greenhouse, coop, barn, cottage_bram, cottage_marrow, cottage_deeps, cottage_sable, cove, summit. Maps `{id,name,width,height,kind:'outdoor'|'indoor'|'mine',connections:string[]}`.
Fish fields `{id,name,locations:string[],seasons:Season[],minSize,maxSize,price,behavior}`. Forage `{id,name,seasons:Season[],locations:string[],price}`. Festivals `{id,name,season,day,activity,description}`. Buildings `{id,name,cost,wood,stone,days}`. Other fields can be added. Catalog owns content consistency and Zod validation tests.
