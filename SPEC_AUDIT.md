# Specification audit

Final source review, 2026-10-23. This replaces the earlier interim audit: the previously listed gameplay omissions have been implemented and must not be repeated as outstanding limitations. Browser and performance claims below remain bounded by the environment in which they were measured.

The generated CONTENT.md now reports 3,326 unique narrative text entries, 81 heart events, nine arcs, sixteen journal pages, four main chapters, and all catalog/audio minimums. The full results in SIM_RESULTS.json record five 300-day runs, each with twenty-four fish species, forty-five sequential arc scenes, ten Sunroots, nine festival types, marriage, a child, and the Thriving ending. These are ordinary-command progression results, not assertions of human play duration.

## Actual remaining deviations and verification limits

| Requirement                                                                    | Evidence and remaining limit                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Check and commit immediately after every milestone (§0, §23)                   | Development was integrated across parallel ownership areas with targeted checks and later integrated verification. The historical sequence cannot be retroactively represented as twenty independently completed, checked, and committed milestones. CHANGELOG.md groups features by milestone without claiming those checks or commits occurred.                                                                |
| Passing Firefox and WebKit projects in the full `pnpm check` chain (§22, §25)  | Firefox/WebKit executables could not be installed from blocked official distribution domains in this cloud environment. Their Playwright projects remain configured. Available Chromium, mobile-viewport, and production-offline results must be reported separately; unavailable engines are not passing tests.                                                                                                 |
| 60 FPS on specified physical laptop and phone hardware (§20)                   | The final formal full-field/rain run measured 19.9608 ms per frame (about 50.1 FPS), below the 60 FPS target, with 1,048 crops and twenty-two scheduled villagers. Its 95th percentile was 33.4 ms. A separate standalone renderer measurement near 60 FPS does not replace this formal result. Physical laptop/phone performance also remains unverified; viewport emulation does not reproduce their hardware. |
| First visit under four seconds on broadband and repeat under 1.5 seconds (§20) | Five final fresh-profile cold-title samples were 0.495, 0.492, 0.459, 0.495, and 4.568 seconds: median 0.495 seconds, but only four of five met the four-second target. The outlier is retained. All five offline repeats passed, with a median of 0.238 seconds. These are localhost measurements, not a direct broadband/device test.                                                                          |
| 25–35-hour human main-story play length (§1)                                   | Five deterministic 300-day simulations establish progression and economic solvency. No human play-time study has been conducted, so that duration target remains a design goal rather than a measured result.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

Production Lighthouse, installability, bundle, and offline measurements are tied to a build hash in PERFORMANCE_METRICS.json. The final build achieved desktop Lighthouse 99, with 0.501-second first contentful paint, 0.705-second largest contentful paint, 70 ms total blocking time, zero layout shift, and no Chromium CDP installability errors. Its exact production index SHA-256 is `45f9c6473e2441d73c1a64af3f8d05f3b10e4b8cbf2acf607de531029322cba9`. Cold-title median was 0.495 seconds, with a retained 4.568-second outlier; only four of five samples met the four-second target. All five offline repeats passed, with a 0.238-second median. The final browser heap test used forced-GC retained size before and after sixty days: 42,921,108 to 43,402,508 bytes, a 1.12% increase, within the 10% limit. Its formal gameplay frame result remains about 50.1 FPS, below the requested target. The final browser run recorded 36 passes and two project-specific skips; 36 Firefox/WebKit failures were caused by missing browser binaries.

## Documentation status

BALANCE.md and SIM_RESULTS.json contain current simulation economics. README.md specifies the installed toolchain’s supported Node versions and separates simulation, browser, offline, load-time, and hardware evidence. CONTENT.md is generated from data rather than manually asserted. CREDITS.md records inspected installed-package license declarations. No documentation here marks unavailable cross-browser checks as successful or claims that the original milestone commit sequence occurred.
